## Overview

Cheerful and vibrant take on neobrutalism with bright colors, bold borders, and a playful, high-contrast aesthetic on clean surfaces.

## Style Foundations

- **Visual style:** cheerful, vibrant, high-contrast, playful
- **Typography scale:** 13/15/17/21/27/35
- **Typography fonts:** primary=Inter, display=Inter, mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Typography weights:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Color palette:** primary, neutral, success, warning, danger
- **Spacing scale:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Colors

- **Primary (#FFD600):** Cheerful and vibrant yellow for main accents.
- **Secondary (#FF90E8):** Playful pink for secondary elements.
- **Success (#00E699):** Bright teal for positive states.
- **Warning (#FFA629):** Vivid orange for attention.
- **Danger (#FF4D4D):** Bold red for alerts.
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** Clean white background for a fresh feel.
- **Text (#1A1A1A):** Soft black for high readability without being too harsh.
- **Neutral (#F5F5F5):** Light grey for subtle backgrounds.
